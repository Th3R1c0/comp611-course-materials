[BS-1] BST rule and height

Every node: left part smaller, right part bigger. Height = number of edges on the longest root-to-leaf path.Example: the path 41, 20, 29, 26 has 4 nodes but 3 edges, so height 3. A root alone has height 0.

A leaf is a node with no children. NIL means an empty child spot.

[BS-2] Walks: inorder, preorder, postorder

Inorder = left, root, right = the keys sorted. Preorder = root, left, right (root comes first). Postorder = left, right, root (root comes last).Example: root 50 with 30 (children 20, 40) and 70. Preorder 50, 30, 20, 40, 70. Postorder 20, 40, 30, 70, 50.

Trace around the outside anticlockwise from the root. Preorder writes a node as you pass its left side, postorder as you pass its right side.

[BS-3] Search, minimum, maximum

Search: smaller go left, bigger go right, until found or you fall off. Minimum = keep going left. Maximum = keep going right.Example: search 32 in root 41: 41, then 20, then 29, then 32.

Each step throws away a whole half of what is left, so it follows only one path.

[BS-4] Successor and predecessor

Successor (next bigger): has a right child, so go right once then left all the way. No right child: climb until you step up from a left child; that parent is it. Predecessor is the mirror.Example: successor of 41 (right child 65) is 50. Successor of 32 (no right child) climbs 29, 20, then 20 is a left child of 41, so 41.

Check with the sorted list: the successor is the key right after it.

[BS-5] Insert and delete

Insert: search for it and attach it as a leaf where you fall off. The first key inserted is the root.Example: insert 30: 41 left, 20 right, 29 right, 32 left, empty, so left child of 32.

Nothing else moves, so insertion order decides the shape.

Delete: 0 children, remove it. 1 child, the child moves up. 2 children, replace with the successor (right once, then left all the way) and remove the successor from its old spot.Example: delete 65 (children 50 and 91): successor is 72, so 72 takes 65's place.

The successor has no left child, so removing it from its old spot is always the easy case.

[BS-6] Running times

Search, insert, delete, min, max, successor: O(h). Balanced: O(log n). Worst case, a chain from sorted inserts: O(n). Full walk: Θ(n).Example: inserting 10, 20, 30, 40, 50 makes a chain of height 4.

Red-black trees (skipped) exist to keep h at O(log n).
